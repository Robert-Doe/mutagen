A History of Mutation XSS2013 → the Sanitizer API

A timeline no single module owns: where the class was named, how the bypass-and-patch cycle ran for a decade, and why the platform's structural answer arrived when it did. Read this after Module 5; it makes every later module land.

☞

Sekondi

Kofi's customs shed didn't get safe in one memo. Someone found a hole; the rulebook got an amendment; someone found the next hole. For years. The amendments piled up until the supervisor realised the procedure itself — seal the crate, send it on, let someone downstream re-open it — was the hole. The fix wasn't another amendment. It was wheeling the goods onto the floor without a crate. That last move is the Sanitizer API. Everything before it is amendments.
